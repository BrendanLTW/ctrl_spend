ctrlWorks · product first

The product symbol is now the logo. Each app is one bold, simple shape in its own colour: a coin, a bill, a house, a play button. What ties them to ctrlWorks, the company, is a small ⌃ caret, the symbol printed on the Control key, cut into every shape. You recognise the product by its shape, and the brand by the caret.
